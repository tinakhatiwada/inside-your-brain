      How it stays honest

      Reviewed notes underneath everything. The 3D brain maps 21 areas, each with plain-language notes and sources. The AI can only light up those areas, and every answer says which notes it used.

      Simplified, and it says so. Real brains work in parallel, not one step at a time. Timings are rough, and every replay ends with the fine print on what’s simplified or still debated.

      Learning, not diagnosis. This explains how brains generally work. It can’t tell you anything about your health. If something worries you, talk to a doctor.

      Inside Your Brain is an educational project. It isn’t medical advice. The 3D brain is based on Frank Johansson’s anatomical brain model.
